Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Tree Hombres LLC ("Tree Hombres," "we," "us") runs treehombres.com. This policy explains what information we collect through the website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request a quote, we collect your name, phone number, property address or town, and anything you tell us about the job. If you call or text us, we receive your phone number and whatever you share with us.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request, come out to look at your trees, give you a quote, and do the work.
  • To keep records of our quotes and jobs.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Information collected automatically

Google Ads. We use the Google tag to measure how our ads perform, such as whether someone who clicked one of our ads later requested a quote or called us. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. For visitors who arrive from one of our ads, the phone number on the site may be replaced with a Google forwarding number that connects the call to us; Google then records call details such as the caller's area code, the time of the call, and how long it lasted. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Microsoft Clarity. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our site, including clicks, scrolling, and session recordings, so we can make the site easier to use. Microsoft collects this data using cookies and similar technologies. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Website hosting. Our website host, Cloudflare, processes basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business: our email providers, which deliver quote requests to us; our website host; and Google and Microsoft for the measurement described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work. You can manage how Google personalizes ads for you at My Ad Center.

How long we keep information

We keep quote requests and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and for our normal business records.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your requests

To ask what information we have about you, or to have us correct or delete it, call us at (512) 887-8722 or email info@treehombres.com. We'll respond within 45 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
