What affects the price

What goes into a tree removal price

Every tree is different. These are the things we look at when we quote your job.

Size of the tree

Height, trunk width, and how big the canopy is. Bigger trees take more time, more cutting, and more cleanup.

Access

Whether we can get to the tree easily, or it's in a backyard behind a gate, on a slope, or tucked into a tight spot.

What's nearby

Trees close to the house, fences, other trees, or power lines often have to come down in sections instead of all at once.

Condition of the tree

Dead, rotten, or storm-damaged trees can be unpredictable, so they take extra care to take down safely.

Haul-off

Want the logs and brush hauled away, or the wood left for firewood? That changes the work involved.

The stump

Let us know what you want done with the stump, and we'll go over the options when we quote.

FAQ

Tree removal cost questions

Why can't you give me a price over the phone?

Two trees that look alike in a photo can be very different jobs once you see where they are and what's around them. We'd rather come out and give you a real price than guess low and change it later.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. We come out, look at your trees in person, and give you a clear price. There's no charge for the quote and no obligation.

Is cleanup included?

Yes. Every job ends with a full cleanup. Whether the logs get hauled off or left for firewood is up to you, and we'll include that in the quote.

Does it cost more if the tree is close to my house?

Usually, yes. Trees near the house, fences, or power lines often have to come down in sections, which takes more time and care than dropping a tree in an open yard.
