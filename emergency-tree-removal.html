FAQ

Emergency tree removal questions

How fast can you get here?

We get out ASAP. Call (512) 887-8722 and we'll tell you when we can be there.

Can you remove a tree that fell on my roof?

Yes. We look at how the tree is resting first, then take it off carefully, in pieces when needed, so we don't cause more damage getting it down.

What if the tree is touching power lines?

Stay away from it and don't try to move anything. Call 911 or your electric company first. Once the utility has made the lines safe, call us to remove the tree.

How much does emergency tree removal cost?

It depends on the size of the tree and the scope of the work. We'll look at it and give you a clear price before we start.

Will my insurance cover it?

That depends on your policy, so check with your insurance company. Take photos of the damage before cleanup starts, and we can give you a written quote to send them.
