FAQ

Common questions

How much does tree service cost?

It depends on the size of the tree and the scope of the work. The quote is free: we come out, look at your trees, and give you a clear price before any work starts. Call (512) 887-8722 or fill out the form to request one.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. We come out, look at your trees in person, and give you a clear price. There's no charge for the quote and no obligation.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. If a tree is down on your house, fence, or driveway, call (512) 887-8722 and we'll get out ASAP. See emergency tree removal.

When do you trim oak trees?

In Central Texas we avoid trimming oaks from February through June, following Texas A&M Forest Service guidance, to protect them from oak wilt. Removals and trimming of non-oak trees happen year-round.

Are you insured?

Yes. Tree Hombres is fully insured.
