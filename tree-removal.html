Tree cutting

Tree cutting and dead tree removal

Tree cutting isn't one-size-fits-all. When there's open room, we can drop a tree whole. When a large tree stands close to your house, fence, driveway, or other trees, we cut it down in sections instead, piece by piece, so it comes down under control and nothing lands where it shouldn't.

Dead trees take extra care. Dead wood gets brittle and can break in ways you don't expect, so we size up the tree and plan the work before we start cutting. Taking a dead tree down on your schedule is usually safer, and a lot less trouble, than cleaning up after it falls on its own.

Every tree removal service we do ends with cleanup. We cut up the trunk and limbs and haul everything away, or leave the logs for firewood if you'd like to keep them. Want the stump gone too? We grind stumps.
