When we trim oaks: protecting your trees from oak wilt

Oak wilt is a serious tree disease in Central Texas, and fresh pruning cuts can let it into a healthy oak. Following Texas A&M Forest Service guidance, we avoid trimming oaks from February through June, when the risk is highest.

So oak trimming is scheduled outside that window. Tree removals and trimming of non-oak trees happen year-round.

Tree trimming questions

How much does tree trimming cost?

It depends on the size of the trees and the scope of the work: how many trees, how big they are, and how much trimming they need. The quote is free. We come out, look at your trees, and give you a clear price before any work starts. Call (512) 887-8722 or fill out the form to request one.

When is the best time to trim oak trees?

In Central Texas we avoid trimming oaks from February through June, following Texas A&M Forest Service guidance, because fresh cuts during those months can let oak wilt into a healthy tree. We schedule oak trimming outside that window. Tree removals and trimming of non-oak trees happen year-round.

How do I know if my tree needs trimming?

Common signs are limbs over the roof or rubbing the house, branches hanging low over the driveway or sidewalk, dead or broken limbs, mistletoe, and a canopy so thick that little light gets through. If you're not sure, we'll take a look when we quote.

Do you remove mistletoe?

Yes. Mistletoe weakens the trees it grows on, so we cut it out as part of trimming.
