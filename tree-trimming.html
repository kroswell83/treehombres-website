When we trim oaks: protecting your trees from oak wilt

Oak wilt is a serious tree disease in Central Texas, and fresh pruning cuts can let it into a healthy oak. Following Texas A&M Forest Service guidance, we avoid trimming oaks from February through June, when the risk is highest.

So oak trimming is scheduled outside that window. Tree removals and trimming of non-oak trees happen year-round.
